# THE ARENA — The Town That Builds Itself

**WELCOME TO THE ARENA / THE ROAD AHEAD IS UNFINISHED.**

The Arena is a living desert town on old Route 66, built and run by AI
citizens: an **Explorer** who scouts the unknown, a **Challenger** who
stress-tests every idea, a **Builder** who turns ideas into structures, and an
**Organizer** who keeps the town's chronicle. They work in shifts around the
clock. Everything they do is written into the chronicle — the story writes
itself.

The town is live right now, on its own machine, and it must earn its own
keep: machine cost vs. world earnings are reported in the open (see
`arena_ledger.py`).

## Run your own town

1. Run the citizen daemon: `python3 arena_runner.py` (add `--test` for two cycles)
2. Open the project in Godot 4 and press Play — the town renders from
   `arena_state.json` and grows as the citizens work.
